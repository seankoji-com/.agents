How this is worked out. Distance is the haversine great-circle distance from the search centre (-33.8330, 151.1830) with an Earth radius of 6371.0 km; stations up to and including 10 km are listed (4 further stations are left out). Only E10 records count. A station's listed price is its most recent E10 record; if two of its records share a timestamp, the one later in the price file counts. A day's average covers stations with a price on or before that day; a day before any in-range price has no average (— in the table, null in data.json). The recommendation is the in-range station with the lowest listed price.
