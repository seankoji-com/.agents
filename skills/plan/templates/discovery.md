# Discovery template

Record the problem, constraints and decisions needed to act without the conversation.
Adapt the sections to the task and repository format; mark drafts and unknowns.

## 1. The problem or decision
State what needs deciding or changing, who is affected and why it matters.

## 2. Requirements and constraints
Record relevant budget, deadline, compatibility, compliance and downstream constraints.
For implementation topics, name actual paths and check commands where known. Separate
confirmed requirements from proposals and assumptions; do not invent missing details.

## 3. Tradeoffs discussed and how each was resolved
Record material choices, evidence, costs and whether each decision is accepted,
proposed or unresolved. Include its acceptance reference and source version where relevant.

## 4. Alternatives considered and ruled out, with reasons
Preserve material rejected alternatives and their reasons so later work does not repeat
settled discussion. Mark superseded decisions and retain their reasoning. Consider a
simpler approach or doing nothing where useful; do not manufacture alternatives to fill
the section.

## 5. Explicit non-goals
Name adjacent work that is excluded and any limits on implementation or delivery authority.

## 6. Prior art
Summarize relevant prior attempts and sources, what they established and what remains
unresolved. Include credible counter-evidence to the proposed approach when available.

## 7. Open questions
Separate execution blockers from routine choices that permit stated assumptions. For
each blocker, record the gate, owner, dependent work to hold and next action. Include
unresolved licence, budget, identity, credential and authority gates. Name unknown
owners as unknown. Continue independent work within scope.
