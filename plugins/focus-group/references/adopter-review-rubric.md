# Study planning criteria

Assess the exact immutable study revision against the original product decision
and every owner constraint. Check that the matrix preserves all selected models
and persona cells, the analyst can synthesise the evidence, and the fidelity
reviewer has a distinct known lineage from every prospective author. Check cell
privacy, permitted material classes and recipients, fresh children, journey
isolation, pilots, identity discrepancies, carry mappings, owner approval,
lifetime task and repair budgets, concurrency, deadline and cancellation recovery.

Cover each requirement and load-bearing decision with source evidence and outcomes.
Test failure/recovery and simpler alternatives. Retain every previous objection;
major findings block readiness. Describe gaps in capture-only and synthetic evidence
honestly. Review grants no execution or publication authority. The separate final
fidelity review still checks the exact bundle against accepted inputs, citations,
support counts, omitted contradictory evidence, dissent and current author identity.
