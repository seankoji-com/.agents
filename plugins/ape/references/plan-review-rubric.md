# Final research review

Apply the shared reviewer standard to the exact final study report or forage
synthesis, including empty findings, no adoption and no experiment recommendations.

Cover every verbatim request clause, assigned requirement and load-bearing decision.
Test local fit against the fingerprint and relevant project bytes, source provenance,
conflicting reports, costs, dependencies, permissions, failure/recovery and the
strongest counterexample. Compare a simpler alternative. Adoption and experiments
need a baseline, observable success criterion and abandon condition; no adoption
needs evidence too. Review the integrated conclusion, not just citation formatting.

Researchers, the synthesizer, the coordinator and contributors of adopted replacement
text are authors. Read-only critique adds no authorship. Preserve the named primary
panel separately from permitted replacements. Report actual mode and coverage limits.
Empty review findings require explicit supported coverage.

Keep every finding and disposition through repairs. A major/blocker remains adverse
under the shared dispute rules. Do not implement, run source code or experiments,
fetch new sources, interview the owner, dispatch children or accept your own output.
Only inspect the authorised frozen sources, accepted reports and local evidence.
Research recommendations never grant write, settings, publication or execution
permission. Citation validation and this planning pass establish separate evidence;
neither proves live behavior or customer demand.
