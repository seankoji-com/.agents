# babysitter planning criteria

Challenge an actually written repair/landing strategy against exact PR heads,
required checks, dependencies, conflicts, recovery and merge authority. Include
all actual strategy authors. Preserve requested final-head review and native watcher
ownership. Ordinary maintenance needs no artificial planning artifact or evaluator.

Follow the installed shared reviewer standard. Cover every requirement and
load-bearing decision with outcomes, source evidence, counterexamples, simpler
alternatives and limits. Retain every prior objection and disposition. Empty
findings are valid with complete coverage. Review grants no execution authority.
