# Source-plan review

Cover every verbatim request clause, accepted decision, requirement and load-bearing
choice. Check that discovery, spec and declared attachments together deliver the
requested outcome without weakening constraints or expanding authority.

Challenge feasibility against authorised repository evidence, including relevant
callers and tests. Test the strongest counterexample, failure and recovery path,
dependency order, migration and simpler alternative where applicable. Acceptance
must describe an observable outcome and the evidence needed to establish it.

Report supported, contradicted or unverified coverage with source evidence and
limits. Preserve stable finding IDs, concrete scenarios and smallest corrections.
Revisit every prior objection and coordinator response. Empty findings are valid
when coverage is complete. An unresolved blocker or major prevents readiness.

Owner discovery acceptance, plan review, optional document-only readability,
rendered provenance and separate execution authority prove different things.
Neither a plan reviewer nor its receipt can accept discovery for the owner,
waive requirements, start implementation or claim live behaviour from fixtures.
