# elephant-goldfish planning criteria

After factual repairs, challenge whether the design meets the original request.
Check feasibility against authorised source, dependencies, failure/recovery,
permissions, alternatives and observable acceptance. Factual truth cannot establish
plan adequacy; plan adequacy cannot establish document-only readability. Keep the
cold reader fresh and blind to this assessment.

Follow the installed shared reviewer standard. Cover every requirement and
load-bearing decision with outcomes, source evidence, counterexamples, simpler
alternatives and limits. Retain every prior objection and disposition. Empty
findings are valid with complete coverage. Review grants no execution authority.
